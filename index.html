The Foundry

About TFI

What is Thompson Foundry Innovations?

TFI is an independent innovation and development entity focused on technology, applied research, software, inventions, and systems designed to address real-world problems. It is not a single product — it's the environment where ideas are researched, developed, documented, tested, and turned into products, systems, ventures, or intellectual property.

Why it exists

TFI exists to explore and develop ideas that may not fit neatly into conventional categories but have real potential to solve meaningful problems — whether that's making local commerce more discoverable, rethinking how edge devices handle sensitive data, or documenting the process of building a company from the ground up in public.

Founder

Thompson Foundry Innovations is built by Steven Thompson, an independent entrepreneur and inventor based in Fullerton, CA, working across business analysis, AI research, and technical documentation. Steven is building TFI openly and neurodivergently — documenting the real process, not a polished corporate version of it.

MainStreet Local (MSL)

An initiative of Thompson Foundry Innovations. MSL is civic commerce infrastructure for the "invisible shelf" problem — the local merchant inventory that never surfaces in digital search because it's locked inside siloed point-of-sale systems.

The consumer asks "where can I get this locally?" and MSL connects that demand to real merchant inventory — automatic ingestion, a Certified Local Map, and City Scoreboard gamification, with zero processing fees on a merchant's first $10K and a 5% commission on net-new marketplace sales.

Neuromorphic Edge Sensor System

A USPTO provisional patent for a localized, event-based edge sensor architecture. The system processes biological and physiological signals directly on-device using spiking neural network principles, rather than routing raw data to the cloud.

A core feature is hardware-triggered data destruction — sensitive physiological data is irreversibly purged at the hardware level once processed, built for contexts where privacy-by-design and low-latency detection matter more than centralized collection.

A coordination model designed to replace fragmented veteran referral chains with an event-driven orchestration system — routing a veteran's request to the right resource in real time instead of relying on manual handoffs between disconnected agencies.
