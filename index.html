01. Information Binding Model (IBM)

THE INFORMATION BINDING MODEL

Reframing Spacetime, Distance, and Consciousness

This framework proposes that reality may be understood as a non-local, unerasable archive of information. TFI utilizes this model to understand data persistence and systemic interoperability.

PILLAR I: THE MASTER LIBRARY

Existence functions like a library where information is organized, indexed, and accessed. Dimensions are coordinates determining where information is displayed.

PILLAR II: SEQUENCED CONTACT MODEL

All dimensions and realities exist in direct sequential contact. Physical distance is an artifact of informational layering.

PILLAR III: THE COSMIC CLICK

The Big Bang is interpreted as a fundamental activation event, the moment the system initialized the universe's code.
